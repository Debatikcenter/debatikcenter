❮ ❯
 

MANIFESTO Great Wave: "TWO HOURS OF FRESH AIR", an unexhibition at ZETA Contemporary Art Center, provided an overview of two decades of artistic activities, found performances of violence, and texts produced since the 2000s by the DebatikCenter of Contemporary Art and Manifesto Collective. By proposing alternatives to the inevitability of neoliberal collusion of art and politics, this marginal archive has become the matrix to shake up today's society.

This unexhibition was conceived by Armando Lulaj and Manifesto Collective and was accompanied by Manifesto Collective's Newspaper containing highlights of the projects together with critical texts from the last decade.
