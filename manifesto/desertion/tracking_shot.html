/TRACKING SHOT



Act written by David Kampi
Executed by Armando Lulaj

1. EXT. SOMEWHERE - DAY. NIGHT

A man walks barefoot, pushing a large, polished stainless steel object. The object occasionally glints in the light of the streetlights that illuminate it. Like a ghost he moves slowly. He passes in front of important institutions and museums of various cities, the Guggenheim Museum in NY, the Fridericianum in Kassel, pushing the empty autopsy table.

Armando Lulaj, TRACKING SHOT, February 7, 2026. First act of Rousing Social Order, Bologna. Photo: Massimo Sciacca. Courtesy of Galleria de'Foscherari and DebatikCenter of Contemporary Art. © D.C.C.A. Archives.
